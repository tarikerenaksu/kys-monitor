# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate respectfully and constructively. Technical disagreements are welcome; personal attacks, harassment and discriminatory behaviour are not.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and professional.
- Focus criticism on the code or proposal, not on people.
- Provide reproducible technical information when reporting problems.
- Protect credentials, personal data and other sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, deliberate exposure of private information, and abusive or discriminatory conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, close or remove contributions that do not follow this code of conduct.
